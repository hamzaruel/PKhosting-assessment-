Frequently asked questions

How do I compare monthly and annual prices?

The pricing cards show the selected billing period and currency. Annual billing covers 12 months of service for the cost of 10 monthly payments. Confirm the final invoice total at checkout.

Which plan should I choose?

Starter is presented for small projects and testing. Growth is for growing applications, Scale for demanding projects, and Dedicated Core for larger workloads. Confirm the plan resources against your needs before ordering.

What specifications are included with each plan?

CPU, memory, storage, networking, backup, and support details are marked for confirmation in the comparison table. Please confirm these specifications before publishing or purchasing.

Which payment methods are available?

The verified options are Visa and Mastercard through Stripe, PayPal, JazzCash, Easypaisa, and bank transfer. Wallet payments are for PKR invoices. PayPal is listed for USD, GBP, and EUR invoices, not PKR invoices. Card and bank transfer currency support can vary by invoice currency.
