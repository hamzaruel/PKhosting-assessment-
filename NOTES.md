# Research notes

## Pricing page audit summary

- The accessible VPS page showed multiple billing terms. Their controls and price changes could not be tested interactively during the audit.
- Pricing cards showed resource details and plan labels. The audit relied on accessible page content rather than interactive browser testing.
- Add-on charges were shown separately from base VPS prices.
- The official FAQ and page footers showed Stripe, PayPal, JazzCash, Easypaisa, and bank account as payment channels.
- The FAQ states that Stripe card payments support Visa and Mastercard.
- Six named payment options were verified when Visa and Mastercard are counted separately: Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, bank transfer, JazzCash, and Easypaisa. The assessment brief mentions eight, but two additional methods could not be verified on the accessible official pages.
